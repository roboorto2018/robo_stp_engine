robo_stp_engine - Assimp (Open Asset Import Library) and the libraries it contains
=================================================================================

The engine reads FBX, glTF/GLB, OBJ, 3MF and PLY files with Assimp 6.0.2 (https://github.com/assimp/assimp), linked as a
static library built with only those importers. Assimp and the parts of its 'contrib' folder that are compiled into it come
with the licenses below. Il motore legge i file FBX, glTF/GLB, OBJ, 3MF e PLY con Assimp 6.0.2, collegato come libreria
statica con i soli lettori di questi formati; Assimp e le librerie della sua cartella 'contrib' compilate con lui hanno le
licenze qui sotto.
