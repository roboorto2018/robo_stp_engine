robo_stp_engine - third-party Rust components / componenti Rust di terzi
=========================================================================

The engine (robo_stp_engine.exe, robo_stp_engine_silent.exe) contains code from the Rust crates below. Where a crate
offers a choice of licenses ("MIT OR Apache-2.0" and similar), it is used here under the MIT license. Open CASCADE
Technology, also linked into the engine, is covered by its own license (see LICENSE and OCCT-EXCEPTION.txt).

Il motore contiene codice delle librerie (crate) Rust qui sotto. Quando una libreria lascia scegliere fra piu' licenze,
qui e' usata con la licenza MIT. Open CASCADE Technology, anch'essa collegata nel motore, ha la sua licenza (vedi LICENSE e
OCCT-EXCEPTION.txt).
